How to read this

Offer value
Cash per share plus the exchange ratio times the acquirer's price. Stock deals move with the acquirer. A CVR is noted, never valued.
Spread
(Offer value minus target price) divided by target price. What is left on the table if the deal closes on its terms. Negative means the stock trades above the offer.
Annualized
Spread times 365, divided by days to the stated expected close. Simple, not compounded. Shown as Unknown when the filings give no close date. When they give a period, we use its last day.
Implied chance (rough)
(Price minus unaffected price) divided by (offer value minus unaffected price), clipped to 0 to 100%. The unaffected price is the close before the announcement (the announcement day itself when the deal came out after the close, or an earlier date when the release names one). It assumes the stock falls back to its unaffected price if the deal breaks, and ignores time value and the chance of a higher bid.
